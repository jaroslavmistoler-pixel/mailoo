Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

In short: pidgoo has no server of its own that your email passes through. Your accounts, passwords, emails, contacts, calendars and settings stay encrypted on your computer only. We collect no data about you or about how you use the app. No ads, no AI, no tracking.

Who is behind pidgoo

pidgoo is a free email app for Windows and Mac made by an independent developer (publisher “mistak”). Contact: hello@pidgoo.com.

What data pidgoo handles and where

pidgoo works with the data you enter or that arrives in your mailboxes: email accounts and sign-in details, messages and attachments, contacts, calendars, tasks, signatures and settings. Everything is stored only on your computer in an encrypted vault (AES-256). The vault key is protected by your operating system (Windows or macOS) or by a password you set in pidgoo. The author of pidgoo has no access to this data and never receives it.

What pidgoo connects to

The app only connects where its features require it, always directly from your computer:

  • Your mail, calendar and contact servers (IMAP, SMTP, JMAP, CalDAV, CardDAV, Google Calendar) that you add yourself. Data is exchanged directly with them.
  • Sign-in with Google or Microsoft – only if you choose it when adding an account. You sign in directly with Google or Microsoft; pidgoo never sees your password.
  • Server settings lookup – when you add an account, pidgoo may look up the domain of your address (the part after @) in DNS and in the public autoconfig.thunderbird.net database. Only the domain is sent, never the full address or password.
  • Updates – the version downloaded from pidgoo.com checks for new versions on GitHub (github.com). The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store. Only an ordinary file download request is sent.
  • Message translation – only if you enable it with your own Google Cloud key and click Translate. The text of the message being translated is then sent to Google Cloud Translation.
  • Unsubscribe – when you click Unsubscribe, pidgoo sends an unsubscribe request to the address provided by the newsletter sender.
  • Remote images in emails are blocked by default. If you allow them for a message, they are downloaded from the sender’s server, which may learn that you opened the message.
  • Links (for example pidgoo.com or Ko-fi for voluntary support) open in your web browser.

What pidgoo does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking code or advertising.
  • It does not send your emails, contacts or other data to the author or any third party (other than the services listed above that you choose to use).
  • It does not use artificial intelligence or use your data to train anything.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you remove it. Remove an account in Settings → Accounts; uninstalling the app also removes its data – automatically for the Microsoft Store version and after confirmation for the Windows version from the website. On a Mac, data stays in ~/Library/Application Support/pidgoo until you delete that folder. Mail on your servers is not affected.

The pidgoo.com website

The website uses no cookies or analytics and serves its fonts itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process access logs (such as IP addresses) for technical and security reasons. The chosen website language is remembered only by your browser.

Children

pidgoo is not directed at children, and the author does not knowingly collect any data from children – the app collects no data from anyone.

Changes
